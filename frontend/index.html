How ScamShield works

Three layers work together, so one weak signal never decides the result on its own.

1. Privacy first

Sensitive numbers such as phone, card, Aadhaar, PAN and UPI IDs are masked in typed text before any analysis.

2. Hybrid detection

A rule engine checks urgency tactics, lookalike domains and risky links, and an AI model reads the full context, including screenshots.

3. Clear guidance

You get a risk score, plain-language reasons, and one-tap actions to report, warn family and protect your accounts.
